---------------------------------------------------------------

**TACZ Bipod** adds weapon mounting to Timeless and Classics Zero, similar to the mounting in recent Call of Duty games. Walk up to cover, mount your gun, and fire with less recoil.

## How it works
Get in position with a gun and a prompt appears. **Hold aim** to mount and let go to stand, or press **B** to toggle mounting so you can aim or hip-fire freely.

- **On corners (any gun):** lean on the edge of a wall, pillar or door frame and your view tilts out around it. Reduced recoil.
- **On top of cover (bipod guns):** full blocks, fences, walls and closed fence gates. Uses the gun's crawl recoil and tighter hip-fire spread.
- **Prone (bipod guns):** crawl and deploy the bipod on the ground for the steadiest fire.

Mounting takes a moment to settle and limits how far you can turn. Move to dismount, and a hard hit knocks you off.

## Choosing which guns have a bipod
Server operators control it in-game:
- `/bipod addheld` / `/bipod removeheld` (the gun in your hand)
- `/bipod attachment addheld` (any gun with that attachment installed counts as having a bipod)
- `/bipod list` · `/bipod reload`

Every TACZ default gun with a bipod on its model has one by default: the M249, RPK, FN Evolys, PKM, HCAR, Mk 14, SCAR Mk20, SPR15HB, AI AWP, MRAD, M95, M107 and HM50B. Everything else is configurable per world.

## Requirements
- Forge 1.20.1 and **[TaCZ] Timeless and Classics Zero** (tested on 1.1.8-hotfix)
- Install on **both server and client**
